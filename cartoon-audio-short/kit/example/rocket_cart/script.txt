He packed eighty-two rockets into one vegetable cart.

The valley's festival was tomorrow night, and the fireworks budget was exactly zero. So the grocer decided to make his own, and push them up the hill himself.

The mayor laughed in the square. He bet the whole town that the cart would blow his moustache clean off. Half the town took the bet. A kid bet his lunch.

Then the rain came. The grocer slipped. The cart did not. Eighty-two rockets went rolling down Main Street on their own, with a grocer chasing them like a lost dog.

A delivery van hit the brakes. The cart hit the fence. The grocer hit the deepest puddle in town.

So what's in there? Eighty-two rockets, and only one fuse, tied in a ring around them. Light it anywhere, and the fire runs both ways at once. Nobody has to light eighty-two of anything.

By midnight he had dragged it up the hill through the storm. The mayor followed, still laughing, still betting. The grocer struck one match.

The whole valley lit up gold. And the mayor lost his bet, and his moustache.
